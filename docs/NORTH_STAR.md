# North Star

> **The most relevant changes in the world, translated into my personal market
> understanding, my industries, my accounts and my next conversations.**

Product promise: **Know what changed, understand why it matters, and be ready to
discuss it.**

## The three obligatory dimensions

Every day the platform must make the user better along three axes. These are
requirements, not slogans: a feature that serves none of them is not built.

### 1. Recency — *what changed?*

- what happened today; what is new since the last visit
- which companies moved (partnerships, products, investment, M&A, research,
  regulation, market shifts)
- which trends are gaining or losing momentum
- which earlier reports were updated, corroborated, corrected or retracted
- what is genuinely new vs. re-communicated
- what was announced vs. what was actually implemented

### 2. Depth — *how does this market really work?*

- how an industry earns money; where value is created and margin is captured
- value chains, business models, customer segments, channels
- the KPIs and value drivers that decide outcomes
- required capabilities and operating models
- which technologies change which part of the model
- structural challenges and the regulatory frame
- how today's event sits in a longer arc, and how the user's own understanding
  develops over weeks and months

### 3. Applicability — *what do I say or ask next?*

- concrete talking points and high-quality questions
- strategic hypotheses and client implications
- risks, counter-arguments, comparable examples
- executive summaries and meeting preparation
- follow-up questions and the next research or learning step

## The connecting rule

The most important experiences must connect all three. A news item about a new AI
partnership is not adequately handled by a summary. The platform must answer:

1. **What exactly was announced?** → Recency
2. **Which larger market, industry or technology pattern does it fit?** → Depth
3. **Which hypothesis, client implication or question follows?** → Applicability

Traceability from feature to dimension to user outcome is maintained in
[FEATURE_TRACEABILITY.md](FEATURE_TRACEABILITY.md).

## What the platform must never claim

It is not omniscient. It must never imply it has captured every relevant development.
Instead it shows, always: which sources are monitored, when each last synchronised
successfully, which regions/languages/topics are covered, which sources are stale or
failing, where the coverage gaps are, how confident a statement is, and whether a
statement is a fact, an interpretation, a hypothesis or a forecast.

"No new results" is rendered as *"No new events were found in the currently monitored
sources"* — never as "nothing happened".

## The measure of a good session

After using it, the user should be: better informed, deeper in their understanding of
the market, able to separate fact from marketing, asking better questions, better
prepared for conversations, aware of their own knowledge gaps, retaining what they
learned — and certain their time was better spent than in a social feed.
