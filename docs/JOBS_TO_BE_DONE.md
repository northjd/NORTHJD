# Jobs To Be Done

Each job states the user's own framing, the trigger, what "done" looks like, and where
it lives in the product.

| #   | Job                                 | User framing                                                                                                                   |
| --- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| J1  | Daily Awareness                     | "Show me in under 15 minutes which developments I actually need to understand today."                                          |
| J2  | Continuous Learning                 | "Help me build systematically deeper market, industry and technology competence over weeks and months."                        |
| J3  | Meeting Preparation                 | "Prepare me in a few minutes for a specific conversation with a company, colleague or executive."                              |
| J4  | Company Monitoring                  | "Show me how a company is changing strategically, technologically and operationally."                                          |
| J5  | Industry Understanding              | "Explain not just the news but how this industry works and where value is created."                                            |
| J6  | Technology & Ecosystem Intelligence | "Show me what relevant technology providers, platforms and ecosystem partners are building, announcing and actually shipping." |
| J7  | Competition Intelligence            | "Show me publicly observable moves by consultancies and other competitors — without the product becoming only that."           |
| J8  | Research                            | "Answer complex market questions from verifiable sources."                                                                     |
| J9  | Knowledge Application               | "Translate information into hypotheses, talking points and better questions."                                                  |
| J10 | Critical Thinking                   | "Challenge my view, show counter-arguments, stop me seeing only confirmation."                                                 |
| J11 | Sharing                             | "Turn my research into a short, executive-ready, citable summary."                                                             |

## Detail

### J1 Daily Awareness

**Trigger** morning, or first open of the day. **Done** the user has read a finite
brief inside their chosen reading budget and reached "You are caught up", having seen at
least one recency item, one depth item and one application item.
**Surface** Today. **Depends on** ranking, event clustering, reading-budget composition.

### J2 Continuous Learning

**Trigger** a knowledge gap surfaced by an event, or a scheduled review.
**Done** a learning unit completed, knowledge state advanced, and the concept connected
to at least one real recent event. **Surface** Learn, Companion _Teach Me_.

### J3 Meeting Preparation

**Trigger** a meeting in the next hours or days. **Done** a brief exists containing a
60-second summary, what changed, verified company context, clearly-labelled hypotheses,
five specific conversation starters, one contrarian angle, known unknowns and sources.
**Surface** Prepare, Companion _Prepare Me_.

### J4 Company Monitoring

**Trigger** a watchlist event, or preparing for an account. **Done** the user can see a
company's timeline, moves, partnerships, technology activity and tracked claims, with
source coverage and open questions stated. **Surface** Explore → Company page.

### J5 Industry Understanding

**Trigger** entering a new industry or sector conversation. **Done** the user can
explain the value chain, business models, profit pools and key KPIs at their chosen
depth level, and locate current events within that model. **Surface** Explore →
Industry page, Learn.

### J6 Technology & Ecosystem Intelligence

**Trigger** an AI/technology announcement, or vendor-selection work. **Done** the user
can distinguish announced from deployed from independently validated, per vendor.
**Surface** Today → AI & Technology Radar, Explore → Technology pages, case maturity.

### J7 Competition Intelligence

**Trigger** competitive positioning or proposal work. **Done** firm moves visible via
the _same generic_ company mechanisms — no dedicated navigation area, no reserved brief
quota, no ranking bonus. **Surface** Explore → Consulting & Professional Services
category.

### J8 Research

**Trigger** an open market question. **Done** an answer structured as executive answer,
verified facts, interpretation, counter-evidence, implications, questions to explore,
sources, coverage and confidence — every factual line navigable to an evidence span.
**Surface** Search, Companion _Explore It_.

### J9 Knowledge Application

**Trigger** after reading an insight. **Done** at least one hypothesis, client
implication or conversation starter saved to the library or a brief.
**Surface** Insight detail, Companion, Prepare.

### J10 Critical Thinking

**Trigger** the user has formed a view. **Done** assumptions named, counter-evidence
retrieved or its absence stated, alternative explanations offered — no synthetic
opposition invented without evidence. **Surface** Companion _Challenge Me_, insight
counter-signals.

### J11 Sharing

**Trigger** a colleague or client needs the conclusion. **Done** an executive summary
or conversation-starter set copied/exported with citations intact.
**Surface** Share actions on insights, briefs and conversations.

## Jobs deliberately not served

Being first to every headline. Complete global coverage. Social interaction between
users. Internal Accenture knowledge management. Sentiment scoring as a substitute for
evidence.
