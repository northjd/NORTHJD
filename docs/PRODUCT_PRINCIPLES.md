# Product Principles

Non-negotiable. When a decision is contested, these decide it.

1. **Evidence before eloquence.** A well-written unsupported sentence is a defect.
2. **Quality before quantity.** Fewer, better items beat comprehensive noise.
3. **Events before articles.** The unit of intelligence is the real-world event, not
   the document that reported it. Ten articles about one announcement are one item.
4. **Impact before popularity.** Never rank by attention.
5. **Context before summary.** A summary without market context is half a product.
6. **Transparency before artificial certainty.** Show confidence, coverage and gaps.
7. **User value before engagement.** No metric that merely rewards longer use.
8. **Finite consumption before infinite scroll.** The daily brief ends. "You are
   caught up" is a real state, and nothing auto-loads after it.
9. **Industry understanding before news accumulation.**
10. **Conversation readiness before information volume.**
11. **Learning over time before temporary information overload.**
12. **Permission-aware access before convenience.**
13. **Primary sources are valuable but not neutral.**
14. **Announcement is not implementation.**
15. **Implementation is not proven impact.**
16. **Build the intelligence layer, not an unlicensed news database.**
17. **Never present a demo as production-ready.**
18. **Never imply monitored sources represent the whole market.**
19. **Accenture is an optional source and company, not the product foundation.**
20. **Voice and text share one evidence and trust model.**
21. **The Companion must deepen thinking, not replace verification.**
22. **The platform should help the user recognise what they do not know.**

## Anti-goals

The product does not: maximise articles shown; maximise time in app; use infinite
scroll; expose public likes or follower counts; gamify pointlessly; rank by
popularity; reproduce press releases uncritically; present corporate marketing as
proven market success; bypass paywalls or auth; store or redistribute content against
terms; infer scale from a pilot, or impact from an announcement; suggest false
completeness; process confidential Accenture or client data without explicit approval;
become an Accenture intranet or a consulting news portal; reduce itself to competitor
watching; or bolt a generic chatbot onto a news app.

## Design principles

Calm, premium, evidence-first, structured. Strong information hierarchy and typography,
adjustable density, restrained visual language, generous whitespace, minimal purposeful
motion. No engagement manipulation, no infinite scroll, no visual noise. Excellent on
desktop and on mobile; installable PWA; light and dark; keyboard-navigable with a
command palette; meaningful loading, empty and error states.

The home screen must not read like a news portal of equal-weight tiles. Priority must
be visible at a glance.
